01

About

I am a Senior Applied Scientist at Amazon, working at the intersection of applied machine learning and emerging foundation-model research.

My focus is on adapting language models to the structure and constraints of specialized domains. I am interested in methods that learn from behavioral and sequential signals, improve decisions through feedback, and connect language with visual and structured data.

My earlier research spans computer vision, digital pathology, sparse representation, and multimodal analysis. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Illinois Chicago.
